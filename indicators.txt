Here are the core metrics, one line each, clean and minimal:

Total Return (%) – overall percentage gain/loss over the backtest
CAGR – annualized growth rate of the strategy
Equity Curve – portfolio value over time
Max Drawdown (%) – largest peak-to-trough loss
Average Drawdown (%) – average size of drawdowns
Sharpe Ratio – return per unit of total volatility
Sortino Ratio – return per unit of downside volatility
Calmar Ratio – CAGR divided by max drawdown
Volatility – standard deviation of returns
Number of Trades – total trades executed

Win Rate (%) – percentage of profitable trades
Average Win – mean profit of winning trades
Average Loss – mean loss of losing trades
Profit Factor – total profit divided by total loss
Expectancy – average profit per trade
Average Trade Duration – average time trades are held
Exposure (%) – percentage of time in the market
